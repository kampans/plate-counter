Privacy

Your entries stay in your browser

Plate counter does not collect the information you enter. Your plate selections, calculator inputs, and ranking details are processed in your browser. The app never uploads or saves those entries.

How Rank works

The Rank page downloads a bundled snapshot of public meet results from OpenPowerlifting. Pressing Rank compares your entry with that snapshot on your device.

Cookies and tracking

The app uses no cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts. The site’s hosting provider may process standard connection details, such as your IP address, when serving pages and data files.

External links

External sites apply their own privacy policies when you follow a link. If you choose to send an email, your email provider handles the message and the recipient receives it.
